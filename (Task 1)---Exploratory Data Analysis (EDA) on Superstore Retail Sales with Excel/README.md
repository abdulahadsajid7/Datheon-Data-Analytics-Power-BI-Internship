## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a Superstore Retail Sales dataset using Microsoft Excel. The objective is to analyze historical sales data, identify business trends, evaluate profitability, and generate meaningful insights that can support data-driven business decisions.

The analysis covers sales performance across different years, months, product categories, sub-categories, geographical regions, and customer segments. It also explores the relationship between discount rates and profitability.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
- Evaluate sales and profit performance across product categories and sub-categories.
- Compare regional sales and profitability.
- Analyze customer segment performance.
- Identify the top 10 customers based on sales.
- Examine the impact of discounts on profit.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Excel Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The project uses a Superstore Retail Sales dataset containing transactional sales information.

| Dataset Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| Total Records | 9,994 |
| Total Columns | 22 |
| Unique Orders | 5,009 |
| Unique Customers | 793 |
| Analysis Period | 2015–2018 |

The dataset includes information about orders, customers, products, sales, quantity, discounts, profit, shipping, and geographical locations.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Monthly & Yearly Sales Trend Analysis
- Analyzed sales performance from 2015 to 2018.
- Examined monthly sales fluctuations.
- Identified the highest-performing year and months.

### 2. Top 10 Customers Analysis
- Identified the top 10 customers based on total sales.
- Compared customer contributions to overall revenue.

### 3. Product Category Analysis
- Compared total sales and profit across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
- Evaluated category-wise business performance.

### 4. Regional Sales Analysis
- Analyzed sales and profit across four regions: West, East, Central, and South.
- Identified the highest-performing geographical region.

### 5. Customer Segment Analysis
- Evaluated sales and profitability across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- Compared the contribution of each customer segment.

### 6. Sub-Category Analysis
- Compared sales and profit across different product sub-categories.
- Identified profitable and loss-making product sub-categories.

### 7. Discount vs. Profit Analysis
- Examined the relationship between discount rates and profitability.
- Identified discount levels associated with negative average profit.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

The analysis revealed several important business findings:

- **Total Sales:** $2,297,200.86
- **Total Profit:** $286,397.02
- **Best Sales Year:** 2018, generating $733,215.26 in sales.
- **Top Performing Region:** West, with $725,457.82 in sales.
- **Top Performing Category:** Technology, generating $836,154.03 in sales and $145,454.95 in profit.
- **Top Customer Segment:** Consumer, contributing $1,161,401.34 in sales.

### Additional Findings

- Furniture generated approximately $742,000 in sales but only $18,451.27 in profit, indicating comparatively lower profitability.
- The Technology category generated the highest profit among the three product categories.
- The Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies sub-categories recorded negative total profits.
- Discount levels of 30% and above generally showed negative average profit in the analyzed data.
