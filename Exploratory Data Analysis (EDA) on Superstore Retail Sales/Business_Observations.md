# Business Observations

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance, profitability, and discount strategies using Microsoft Excel.

## 2. Key Business Observations

- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Identify profitable and loss-making discount levels.
- Evaluate overall business performance.

## 3. Sales Trend Analysis

The monthly sales trend helps identify changes in sales performance over time.

## 4. Discount vs Profit Analysis

Higher discount levels can reduce profitability. Some discount levels may result in negative profit.

## 5. Business Recommendations

- Avoid excessive discounts that reduce profitability.
- Focus on profitable sales strategies.
- Monitor monthly sales performance.
